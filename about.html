Who We Are

Meteur Online Shopping is built as a customer-focused online marketplace where shoppers can discover products, compare prices, and conveniently manage their purchases.

Our goal is to create a clean, reliable, and easy-to-use shopping experience for customers across Kenya.

What We Offer

Our marketplace brings together different product categories including electronics, phones and tablets, fashion, shoes, beauty, home appliances, computing, groceries, and accessories.

Customers can browse products, view product details, add products to their cart, manage a wishlist, and proceed through the checkout experience.
