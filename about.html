About Meteur

Meteur Online Shopping is a Kenyan e-commerce marketplace designed to make online shopping simple, convenient and accessible for customers.

Our Story

Meteur was created with a simple idea: make it easier for customers to discover products online and enjoy a straightforward shopping experience.

The marketplace brings different product categories together in one convenient online destination, from electronics and computing to fashion, home products, accessories and everyday essentials.

Our goal is to continue developing Meteur into a reliable digital marketplace that connects customers with products and sellers across Kenya.

What We Offer

Meteur provides an online shopping experience covering a growing range of product categories. Customers can browse products, compare available options, add products to their cart and proceed through the shopping process online.
