Shopping on Meteur

The basic shopping process is designed to be simple:

  • Browse products through the Shop page.
  • Select a product to view its details.
  • Add products to your shopping cart.
  • Review your cart before checkout.
  • Enter delivery information.
  • Select an available payment method.
  • Confirm your order.

Real production ordering and payment services will be connected when the backend system is implemented.

Customer Account Help

Meteur includes several customer account pages prepared for future secure authentication.

  • Create an account using the registration page.
  • Verify your email address when email verification is connected.
  • Log in to access your customer account.
  • Manage your profile and delivery addresses.
  • View orders and saved wishlist products.
  • Use password recovery if you forget your password.

The current demonstration uses front-end interfaces and browser storage. Secure production authentication will be added later.

Security Reminder

Protect your personal and payment information when using online services.

  • Never share your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Never share your account password with another person.
  • Avoid entering real payment information into the current demonstration website.
  • Contact support if you notice suspicious activity associated with an account or order.
