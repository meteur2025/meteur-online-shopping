Privacy Policy

Your privacy and information are important to us.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Meteur Online Shopping respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information provided when using our website. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you interact with our marketplace.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order information
  • Account information
  • Information you provide when contacting support

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide and improve shopping services, process orders, communicate with customers, provide customer support, and improve the functionality of the marketplace.

4. Account Information

Customers may eventually be able to create accounts, manage profiles, save delivery addresses, maintain wishlists, and view order history.

Secure account authentication and database storage will be connected as the platform develops.

5. Payment Information

Meteur Online Shopping is currently a front-end demonstration website. Real payment processing has not yet been connected.

Do not enter real payment credentials, M-Pesa PINs, passwords, or other sensitive financial information into this demonstration website.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

The current demonstration may use browser local storage to support features such as shopping carts, wishlists, account demonstrations, and delivery addresses.

Information stored in browser storage can remain on the customer's device and should not be considered a secure replacement for a production database.

7. Information Security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect customer information. Before this website is used for real customer transactions, production-level authentication, database security, payment security, and other appropriate safeguards must be implemented.

8. Third-Party Services

Future versions of Meteur Online Shopping may integrate services for authentication, payment processing, hosting, analytics, delivery, or other marketplace functions. Information shared with such services will be handled according to the applicable service terms and privacy practices.

9. Customer Rights and Requests

Customers who have questions about information associated with their account or interactions with Meteur Online Shopping can contact customer support.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Meteur Online Shopping develops new features and services. Updated versions will be published on this page.
