Privacy Policy

Learn how Meteur Online Shopping handles customer information and protects your privacy.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Meteur Online Shopping respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you when using Meteur Online Shopping.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services you use, Meteur may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery and billing information
  • Account information
  • Order and transaction information
  • Customer support communications
  • Website usage information

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide and improve Meteur services, including:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts
  • Processing orders
  • Providing delivery services
  • Communicating about orders and services
  • Providing customer support
  • Improving website functionality
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Maintaining website security

4. Account Information

If you create a Meteur account, you may provide information such as your name, email address and phone number.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and should contact support if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

5. Orders and Payments

When you place an order, information necessary to process and deliver that order may be collected.

Payment information:

Payment processing may be handled through third-party payment providers. Meteur does not intend to store sensitive payment credentials such as payment card PINs.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Meteur may use browser technologies such as cookies or local storage to support website functionality.

These technologies may help remember shopping cart information, preferences and other website settings.

7. Information Sharing

Meteur may share information with service providers when necessary to provide requested services.

Examples may include payment processors, delivery providers, hosting services and technical service providers.

Information will not be shared for unrelated purposes without an appropriate reason or legal basis.

8. Data Security

Meteur takes reasonable measures to help protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements and protect the security of the platform.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You can contact Meteur support if you would like assistance with a privacy-related request.

11. Children's Privacy

Meteur Online Shopping is intended for general consumers and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

12. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for authentication, hosting, payments, analytics, communications or other technical functions.

Those services may have their own privacy policies governing how information is handled.

13. Changes to This Policy

Meteur may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, technology or legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised update date.

Privacy Questions?

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Meteur customer support team.
