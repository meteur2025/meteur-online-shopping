Returns & Refunds

Find information about returning products, requesting refunds and getting help with your Meteur orders.

Our Returns Policy

At Meteur Online Shopping, we want customers to have a clear and straightforward process when they need assistance with an order.

Return eligibility may depend on the product, condition of the item, reason for return and the applicable seller or marketplace policy.

Important: Please check the product-specific return conditions before sending an item back. Some products may have different return requirements.
📦

Product Returns

Eligible products may be returned when they meet the applicable return conditions.

💰

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed according to the applicable payment and refund process.

📞

Customer Support

Our support page can help you with questions about returns, refunds and orders.

When Can You Request a Return?

A return request may be appropriate when an eligible product has an issue covered by the applicable return policy.

  • The item arrived damaged.
  • The item is defective.
  • The wrong product was delivered.
  • The product does not match the applicable listing.
  • Another approved reason applies to the order.
Return eligibility is reviewed according to the product and circumstances of the individual order.

Return Conditions

To help a return request be processed smoothly, customers should keep the product in appropriate condition.

  • Keep the original packaging where possible.
  • Include relevant accessories and parts.
  • Do not intentionally damage or modify the item.
  • Provide accurate information about the reason for return.
  • Follow the return instructions provided by support.

Items That May Have Special Conditions

Certain products may have specific return requirements because of their nature, condition, hygiene requirements, warranty arrangements or other applicable restrictions.

Examples can include personal-use products, opened software or digital products, customized products, perishable goods and products with specific warranty requirements.

The applicable product or seller policy should be checked before requesting a return.

How to Request a Return

Follow these general steps when requesting a return:

1
Contact Support

Tell us about the order and the reason for your return request.

2
Provide Details

Provide the requested order and product information.

3
Follow Instructions

Follow the return instructions provided after review.

4
Refund Review

Once approved, the applicable refund process can begin.

Refund Information

Refunds are subject to approval and the applicable payment provider's processing procedures.

The time required for a refund to appear may vary depending on the payment method and financial institution involved.

Please note: A refund request does not automatically mean that a refund will be approved. The order and return circumstances may need to be reviewed first.

Damaged or Incorrect Products

If your order arrives damaged or you receive a different product from the one ordered, contact customer support as soon as reasonably possible.

Where appropriate, photographs or other information may be requested to help review the issue.

Return Shipping

Return shipping arrangements may vary depending on the reason for the return, product type, seller policy and delivery arrangements.

Customers should wait for return instructions before sending products to an address that has not been confirmed by Meteur or the relevant seller.

Exchanges

Where available, an eligible product may be exchanged instead of refunded. Exchange availability depends on product stock and the applicable seller or marketplace policy.

Changes to This Policy

Meteur may update this returns and refunds information from time to time to reflect changes to services, marketplace operations or applicable requirements.

Need Help With a Return?

Contact Meteur customer support if you need help with an order, return request, refund or delivery issue.
