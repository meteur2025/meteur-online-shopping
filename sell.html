What You Need to Sell

Sellers should be prepared to provide accurate information about themselves and their products.

  • Accurate seller or business information.
  • Valid contact information.
  • Clear product names and descriptions.
  • Accurate product prices and availability.
  • Good-quality product images where applicable.
  • Information required for order fulfillment.
  • Compliance with applicable marketplace requirements.
Seller responsibility: Sellers are responsible for ensuring that their product listings are accurate and that their products comply with applicable laws, regulations and marketplace requirements.
