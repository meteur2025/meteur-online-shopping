Shipping & Delivery

Learn how Meteur orders are prepared, shipped and delivered to customers.

Delivery Information

Meteur Online Shopping connects customers with products from our marketplace. Delivery arrangements may vary depending on the product, seller, destination and delivery option selected during checkout.

The estimated delivery information shown during the shopping or checkout process should be used as the primary guide for an individual order.

Important: Delivery estimates are not guaranteed arrival times. Delays may occur because of weather, traffic, public holidays, courier operations, remote destinations or other circumstances outside the normal delivery process.

Delivery Estimates

Delivery times can differ based on the destination, product availability, seller location and courier arrangements.

Delivery Area Typical Estimate Important Note
Major urban areas 1–5 business days Depends on product and courier
Other towns 2–7 business days May require additional transit time
Remote areas 3–10 business days Delivery availability may vary
These are general planning estimates for the website experience and should not be treated as guaranteed delivery times. The checkout estimate for a specific order may be different.

Shipping Costs

Shipping charges may vary according to the product, destination, package size, seller and available delivery method.

Where applicable, the delivery charge will be displayed during checkout before the order is completed.

Delivery Address

Customers should provide an accurate and complete delivery address when placing an order.

  • Provide the correct phone number.
  • Provide accurate location or address details.
  • Include useful delivery instructions where available.
  • Keep your phone available when delivery is expected.
An incorrect or incomplete address may cause delivery delays, failed delivery attempts or additional delivery arrangements.

Failed Delivery Attempts

If a courier cannot complete delivery because the recipient is unavailable or the delivery information cannot be confirmed, another delivery attempt or alternative collection arrangement may be required.

Customers should follow the instructions provided by the relevant delivery service or Meteur support team.

Delayed Orders

If your order takes longer than the estimated delivery period, check the available tracking information first.

If tracking information is unavailable or you need assistance, contact Meteur customer support with your order details.

Damaged Packages

If a package appears damaged when you receive it, document the condition where possible and contact customer support as soon as reasonably possible.

Photographs or other information may be requested to help investigate the delivery issue.

Order Tracking

When tracking information is available, customers can use their order details to check the latest delivery status.

Tracking updates may take some time to appear after a package has been dispatched.
