Shipping Information

Delivery information for Meteur Online Shopping customers.

Last updated: September 2026

Reliable Delivery

Orders will be delivered to supported locations.

Kenya Delivery

Order Tracking

1. Delivery Overview

Meteur Online Shopping is designed to provide customers with convenient delivery options for products purchased through the marketplace.

The current website is a front-end demonstration. Real delivery services and logistics integrations will be connected before production transactions are enabled.

2. Delivery Locations

Future production delivery services may be available to selected locations throughout Kenya.

Delivery availability may depend on the customer's location, seller arrangements, product availability, and the delivery service supporting the order.

3. Delivery Address

Customers should provide an accurate delivery address when placing an order.

The address may include:

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • County
  • Town or city
  • Specific delivery location
  • Additional delivery instructions

4. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges may depend on factors such as delivery location, package size, product type, seller arrangements, and the selected delivery service.

Any applicable delivery charge should be displayed before an order is confirmed in the production checkout system.

5. Delivery Times

Delivery times may vary depending on the customer's location, product availability, seller processing time, courier operations, weather, public holidays, and other circumstances.

Estimated delivery information will be provided through the production ordering system when available.

6. Order Processing

Orders may require processing before they are handed over to a delivery service.

Customers may receive order confirmation and future tracking information when the production order system is connected.

7. Tracking an Order

Meteur Online Shopping includes an order tracking interface that is prepared for future real-time tracking integration.

You can visit the tracking page here:

Track Your Order

8. Delayed Deliveries

Delivery may occasionally be delayed because of circumstances outside the direct control of the marketplace or delivery provider.

Customers should contact support if they need assistance with a delayed order.

9. Delivery Problems

If an order arrives damaged, incomplete, incorrect, or has another delivery-related problem, customers should contact customer support as soon as possible.

Information about returns and refunds is available on our Returns & Refunds page.
